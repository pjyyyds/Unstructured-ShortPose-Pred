# Five-seed matched-history experiment

The seed-42 results at the four PINNS sites were known when this extension was registered. The other four matched-duration training seeds had yet to run. The study concerns the same 623 nonoverlapping forecast origins, 375 within-clip tracks and 47 clips. All three observed histories end at the same time and predict the same 30 successive annotated positions. The 449 origins at the first sampled annotation contribute no windows to this cohort.

The seed list is taken directly from the proposal, in order 42, 1024, 2025, 3407 and 4096. One new Kaggle CPU job per additional seed fits four architectures at three durations across four held-out sites. The durations are five, ten and twenty consecutive frames, spanning 0.4, 0.9 and 1.9 s. The identical historical caches, train and test IDs, uniform sample shuffling, 28-epoch budget and terminal weights apply in every fit. Within each new seed the four architectures and durations share one worker. Retain the separately registered seed-42 results, preferentially use its common-worker 20-frame repeat, and document computer/worker identity for every cell. If the common-worker repeat fails, display that fact and report the original mixed-worker cell separately.

The primary test is the difference in top-probability ADE between the explicit quality gate and motion alone at five frames. The endpoint is the arithmetic mean of the five within-seed differences, giving the four sites equal weight and windows within each site equal weight. Report all five individual seed differences, the five-seed range, site counts and site estimates. For a conditional interval, average paired errors across seeds for the same window, then resample whole clips within each of the four fixed sites 20,000 times with seed 2026 and retain all windows of each sampled clip. This interval conditions on these sites and five fits per site. It does not model sampling of new sites or training seeds.

Report the remaining durations, 3.0 s FDE, implicit-gate and projected-concatenation controls, and history-length changes as descriptive comparisons. Include both last-pair constant velocity and the whole-window least-squares velocity. Report the predefined endpoints for every available seed even when the results favor a baseline. Keep the original 1,482-window five-frame experiment separate from this matched-history extension.
