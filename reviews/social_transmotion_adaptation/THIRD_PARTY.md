The forecasting network and training loss originate from **Social-Transmotion: Promptable Human Trajectory Prediction**, by Saeed Saadatnejad, Yang Gao, Kaouther Messaoud and Alexandre Alahi, Visual Intelligence for Transportation laboratory, EPFL, ICLR 2024. The downstream time-dimension adapter, dated 2026-11-25, changes the JRDB model's fixed temporal lengths from 9 observations/21 total to 5 observations/35 total. The training and input adapter provide PINNS target-only cohorts, two observed-input conditions, a fixed 28-epoch training schedule, and separate forecast scoring. These modifications and the adjacent adaptation drivers are made available under the same GNU AGPL v3. The pinned author model is fetched from its original repository, verified by digest and transformed at runtime. The returned package contains the transformation and the files needed to identify or recreate its source. No original author datasets or weights are included or required. Dataset rights remain with the original licensors.
