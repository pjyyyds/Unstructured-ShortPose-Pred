## Predict from five observations

Run the CPU predictor with a five-frame held-out PINNS checkpoint and an observation JSON file. Use `python -m scripts.predict_five_frame_pedestrian M1.pt five_observations.json --output forecast.json` from the repository root. The inference entry uses NumPy and PyTorch. It accepts the original five-frame M0/M1 weights or a checkpoint from the registered individual joint-action experiment.

Supply five ascending `timestamps_s` at 10 Hz and five measured, consistently oriented, metric `ground_xy_m` positions for one tracked person. Pose models also take `image_height_px` and five `pose_frames`. Each pose frame contains `bbox_center_xywh_px` (box centre x/y, width and height in original image pixels) and `keypoints_xyc` (17 COCO joints with image x/y and confidence). A null pose frame denotes missing image observations. A measured box may be provided with null keypoints when joint estimation is unavailable. Camera calibration and pixel-to-ground conversion are upstream of this entry.

The output contains six future paths of 30 positions at 0.1 s intervals in the input ground coordinate frame, the six probabilities, the zero-based selected path and the last-pair constant-velocity reference. A checkpoint trained with an action head additionally returns five independent event probabilities for start, stop, left turn, right turn and maintain. These targets describe movement events derived from ground trajectories. The four sites have already participated in research development.

Both entry points return `action_coordinate_convention`. In the archived PINNS track coordinates, `Turn-Left` denotes a positive change in the smoothed velocity heading `atan2(v_y, v_x)` and `Turn-Right` a negative change. The PINNS bird’s-eye calibration declares `u_axis=x, v_axis=-y`. Expressing an archived displacement in those declared axes as `(dx, -dy)` reverses the signed turn of the same path. Both predictors preserve the supplied metric axes. Interpreting either checkpoint label as the pedestrian’s physical left or right requires independent confirmation of the ground-axis orientation and direction of travel.

Supervised checkpoints report both the raw action probabilities and a history-only version. The latter sets Start to zero when pre-event speed exceeds 0.20 m/s and Stop to zero below 0.60 m/s. The pre-event speed is the mean of the final three causally smoothed ground speeds. The forward pass and candidate selection precede this reporting step.

For input directly from five camera images, supply timestamps, five tracked center-width-height boxes for one pedestrian and a camera-to-ground homography in metres. The image runner reads the original pixels, estimates 17 RTMPose joints and projects the bottom centre of each box into the supplied coordinate frame. A registered joint-action checkpoint produces six trajectories and five event probabilities. The input schema and time, calibration and image checks are in [the image runner](scripts/predict_five_frame_images.py).

```sh
python -m scripts.predict_five_frame_images detached.pt camera_input.json end2end.onnx --output forecast.json --observations-out observations.json
```

## Benchmark status

Of 1,482 complete PINNS windows, 323 began at the pedestrian’s first sampled annotation in the clip and had cumulative five-frame movement below 0.5 m. Across five fixed seeds and four equally weighted sites, ADE was 0.3545 m for last-pair constant velocity, 0.4130 m for the motion network and 0.4388 m for the pose network. Adding the pose branch raised ADE by 25.78 mm (paired clip-bootstrap 95% interval 16.43 to 35.62 mm). Visibility before the first annotation is unknown. [Protocol](reviews/pinns_short_displacement/PROTOCOL.md) · [Subgroup metrics](reviews/pinns_short_displacement/short_displacement_scores.json) · [Independent verification](reviews/pinns_short_displacement/independent.json)

The archived V1 and V2 Kaggle runs each cover 50 configurations and 242 training runs. In each version, 46 configurations use five seeds and four sensitivity settings use three. V2 reused the split on which V1 had already been inspected. JRDB now has a two-frame 2.5 Hz development comparison. Independent testing under the original five-frame 10 Hz protocol remains a separate requirement. Under the original proposal, the data do not establish A (improvement at all three window lengths) or B (improvement in both short-window quality groups). C applies to the comparisons without a net benefit, including M1 versus its implicit-gate control at five frames.

- On PINNS at five frames, mean top-mode displacement error (ADE, lower is better) was 0.5295 m for the full model and 0.4856 m for the motion-only baseline. On JTA the corresponding errors were 1.8637 m and 1.6640 m.
- There is a narrower result for pose fusion. In 193 JTA test windows with injected heavy noise, M1 reduced mean ADE from 2.3063 m (M1a) to 1.5138 m. The motion-only baseline reached 1.4397 m. M1a adds the motion and available pose embeddings elementwise with equal weight, then applies LayerNorm. M1 scales the pose residual by a quality-dependent gate.
- PINNS contains 47 clips from four sites. The 7,283 windows include 497 test windows at the same sites. JTA uses a separate within-dataset training run and simulated pose corruption. Three external model names in the tables identify local adaptations. Official implementations and independent camera sites still require evaluation.
- A crop check used one frame from each of three PINNS sites, with 13 person boxes of height at least 60 pixels. Moving the crop up by half its height increased model confidence. No manually labeled joint locations were used to measure localization accuracy.
- Among the 323 first-annotation windows with less than half a metre of observed travel, there were 9 starts and 15 stops. At seed 42 the top-ranked path in both models missed every start. At least one of six paths started in 6/9 true starts for M0 and 7/9 for M1, and in 22/314 negatives for each model. For stops, the top-ranked paths hit 2/15 and 3/15 with 2 and 1 false positives. The small counts refer to these development clips. [Candidate generation, ranking and false positives](reviews/pinns_short_displacement/event_coverage/RESULTS.md)
- The historical Tesla T4 mean of 14.488 ms (p95 15.110 ms) used twelve copies of a constant synthetic input, five history frames and newly initialized weights. Two source-verified camera instants each have twelve distinct people, observed in the US and Japan. The new timing protocol feeds their complete inputs to the same trained model and measures predict-only wall time on the identified GPU. [Historical timing audit and synchronized input protocol](reviews/pinns_latency/README.md)
- An optional inference transform aggregates joint features before each bias-free neighbor projection. In the full model at 12 people and 20 frames it reduces the original matrix-and-convolution count from 798,230,016 to 781,306,176. Including elementwise floating-point adds, subtracts and multiplies gives 807,563,628 and 790,639,788 respectively. These counts omit normalization, reductions and activation costs. Under four synthetic input patterns at three lengths, the largest trajectory-coordinate change in CPU FP32 was below one micrometre. [Transform, registered comparison and measured counts](reviews/pinns_pose_compute/README.md)

**PINNS map coordinate correction.** All 47,555 released car rows pair BEV pixels with ground positions. Applying the original contour conversion to the same vehicle centres gives median / P95 discrepancies of 11.19 / 23.86 m because it reverses the vertical axis used by the trajectory labels. Forecasts involving the old image maps need to be interpreted in the historical coordinate convention. The four-site motion-only and pose-only predictors did not read these maps. [Direct source check, geometry scope and reproduction](reviews/pinns_projection_sources/README.md)

On Kaggle, fixing the vertical axis reduced causal ridge `image_geometry` test ADE by 1.86 cm (`0.6234 -> 0.6048 m`), removing ~80% of the inverted-axis penalty; in paired neural `M2a` (`scriptVersionId=373962571`), `real_xy - zero_xy` was +0.0029 m ADE (95% clip CI −0.0169 to +0.0307 m) and −0.0103 m last-valid FDE.
